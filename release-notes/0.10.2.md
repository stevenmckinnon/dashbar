- On a Mac with a touch display (the MacBook Pro with a Dynamic Island), Dashbar's island now starts off for new installs, so it stays out of the way of the Dynamic Island. Settings → Notifications says why, and you can turn it on anyway. A choice you've already made is kept.
- On a touch display, the panel shows the open and stop buttons on servers and the Trash button on clean-up folders without waiting for a hover, and its small buttons are easier to tap. Nothing changes on other Macs.
