- Going back to a session now opens the session itself, not just its app: in Orca, that session's workspace; in T3 Code, its thread; in the Claude app, its Code conversation. Click a session, a notification or the island, or press the jump shortcut. If an app doesn't take the link, it still comes forward as before.
- Workspaces get a button for the app their agent runs in, such as Orca, T3 Code or the Claude app, beside the editor and terminal ones. In the panel's Now card it shows when the card has more than one session; with one, clicking the session does the same.
- Sessions in Orca's terminal panes are now recognised as Orca's. Before, clicking one did nothing useful.
- Small polish on the action tiles: the icons read as one size, and a session's hover highlight has room around its text.
