- Weekly plan limits now show today's share: what was left at midnight, spread over the days until the reset, so you can see how much you can use today and still last the week. On the Usage tab the bar draws today's use lighter, with a tick where today's share ends, and the line under it reads like "Today 6% of about 21%". The History page says the same under each weekly chart. 5-hour limits are unchanged.
- A new notification, off by default (Settings → Notifications → Today's share of weekly limits), tells you once a day when a weekly limit has used today's share, so you can ease off early. Going on is fine; tomorrow's share is just smaller.
- `dashbar status` adds today's share to weekly limits, and `dashbar status --json` has `todayShare` and `usedToday` for them. The dashbar-status skill tells agents to mention it before heavy work.
